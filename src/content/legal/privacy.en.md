## 2. Scope of data collected

In providing its services, the Application collects and processes:

1. **User Account data:**
   * Email address (required for identification, authentication and sending transactional notifications),
   * Account identifier (UUID) assigned within the database,
   * Encrypted access password (managed and protected through Supabase Auth).
2. **Pet Profile data:**
   * Identifying information and characteristics: name, species, breed, date of birth / age, sex, weight and microchip number,
   * Medical and preventive care history: records of vaccinations, deworming, procedures, allergies, medications, past illnesses and individual care notes.  
   *(Data concerning animals does not constitute personal data under Article 4(1) GDPR, but is subject to the same confidentiality standards as an integral part of the User Account's contents)*.
3. **Attachments and documentation:**
   * Pet photos, scanned veterinary discharge summaries, laboratory test results, X-ray images and invoices, which may contain third-party data (e.g. details of a veterinarian or veterinary practice).
4. **Technical and usage data:**
   * IP addresses, session identifiers, access logs, browser type, device operating system and performance metrics generated during communication with the network infrastructure.

## 4. Use of artificial intelligence (Google Gemini API)

1. The Application provides analytical features supported by multimodal artificial intelligence models in the **Google Gemini** family (provided by Google Cloud / Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC).
2. The AI module is activated only as a result of a direct User action (e.g. requesting that a veterinary discharge summary be read or vaccination dates extracted from an attached scan).
3. **No training of public models:** Data is processed through a paid application programming interface (API). In accordance with the technology provider's data processing policy, requests (prompts), photos and documents sent to the Google Gemini API **are not used to train or improve public artificial intelligence models**.
4. All suggestions, extracts and summaries generated by the AI module are provided solely as an aid and do not constitute a veterinary diagnosis.
